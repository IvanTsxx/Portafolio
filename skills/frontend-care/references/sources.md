## Update procedure
The notes are still in progress. To refresh: re-fetch the (private) parent page, diff
against the reference files here, regenerate the affected `references/*.md` plus
`checklist.md` and `assets/cheatsheet.md`, then bump `metadata.version` in `SKILL.md`.

The private source IDs live in `references/sources.local.md` (git-ignored, never
committed) — not in this repo.
