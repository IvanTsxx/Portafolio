---

Navigation uses a single `--dur-portal` window. Scramble, crossfade, and page paint are slices inside that budget — not independent timelines fighting each other.

## Reparto

- **0–120ms** — overlay fade-in (`--dur-fade`)
- **200–700ms** — route-name scramble
- **800ms** — `router.push`
- **900ms** — overlay idle
- **Reduced-motion** — 240ms fade only, no scramble

<Callout tone="dim" title="Cancelation">
Double-navigation aborts the in-flight portal. The second destination wins — no stacked overlays.
</Callout>

## Why not Motion timelines for everything

Chose Motion for interruption-safe portal orchestration (`AnimatePresence` / programmatic control). Local chrome (nav invert, clip-reveal) stays CSS tokens so LCP text does not depend on JS.
